## 1. Executive summary

The platform is a Cognitive Source of Truth for data-centre assets, dependencies, telemetry, energy, cooling and sustainability evidence. It combines a native graph, time-series ingestion, power and battery supervision, liquid-cooling operations, multi-tenant allocation, a 3D/AR interface, and local AIOps.

This revision makes European reporting, PUE/WUE label readiness, waste-heat evidence, CDU monitoring, and tenant-level sustainability reporting Must-Have capabilities. It also corrects two regulatory interpretations from the source brief:

- the adopted EU rating scheme provides separate A-G ratings for PUE and WUE; the official label is generated by the European database, not by the DCIM platform;
- the 10/15/20% heat-reuse targets are German EnEfG requirements, not universal EU thresholds.

The authoritative legal assumptions and source links are maintained in the [Regulatory Compliance Baseline](regulatory-compliance.md).

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 In scope

- Graph-based physical, electrical, cooling, logical, application and tenant topology.
- Agentless discovery and governed computer-vision observations.
- Power topology, power quality, capacity and battery-cell supervision.
- Air and liquid cooling, including CDU and heat-recovery monitoring.
- EED/Regulation 2024/1364 data capture, KPI calculation, validation and export.
- Separate PUE and WUE A-G preview calculations and official-label registry.
- Waste-heat feasibility, readiness, recovery and reuse evidence.
- Renewable-energy instruments and optional hourly energy matching.
- Multi-tenant metering, allocation, partial PUE and CSRD/ESRS evidence exports.
- WebGL digital twin, WebXR field views and real-time subscriptions.
- On-premises AIOps, prediction, RAG and natural-language assistance.

### 2.2 Out of scope

- Legal advice, statutory assurance or automatic certification of compliance.
- Creation of an artifact presented as the official EU PUE/WUE label.
- Firmware flashing.
- Autonomous high-voltage switching or other safety-critical control without approved human intervention.
- Automated filing to a competent authority until the connector, authorization and review workflow have been approved.

## 4. Must-Have functional requirements

### 4.1 Cognitive Source of Truth

- **FR-CSOT-001:** The system shall model sites, buildings, rooms, racks, devices, applications, power paths, cooling loops, CDUs, meters, sensors, tenants, contracts, reporting periods and evidence as versioned nodes and relationships.
- **FR-CSOT-002:** Every discovered fact shall retain source, observation time, confidence and reconciliation state.
- **FR-CSOT-003:** Users shall traverse upstream/downstream power, network and cooling dependencies and calculate blast radius.
- **FR-CSOT-004:** Historical topology shall remain queryable for any locked reporting period.
- **FR-CSOT-005:** Conflicting observations shall enter a review workflow rather than silently overwriting authoritative data.

### 4.2 Telemetry and data quality

- **FR-TEL-001:** The platform shall ingest gRPC, Modbus TCP, BACnet/IP, Redfish, SNMPv3, LLDP/CDP and approved eBPF data through isolated collectors.
- **FR-TEL-002:** Every sample shall preserve value, canonical unit, source unit, source point, timestamps, quality, estimation status and calibration reference.
- **FR-TEL-003:** The system shall detect gaps, duplicates, clock drift, implausible values and unit conflicts.
- **FR-TEL-004:** Aggregates shall expose completeness, estimated coverage and contributing sources.
- **FR-TEL-005:** Corrections shall be append-only and traceable to the original observation.

### 4.3 EED and Regulation 2024/1364 reporting

- **FR-EED-001:** For each facility and reporting period, the system shall record installed IT power demand, rated energy input, country, operator role, exemptions and a reviewable applicability decision.
- **FR-EED-002:** The data dictionary shall cover the information and KPIs required by the active version of Regulation (EU) 2024/1364 Annexes I-III.
- **FR-EED-003:** PUE, WUE, ERF, REF, LEEF when applicable, and other regulated calculations shall use effective-dated formula, unit, boundary and rounding rules. Total water and freshwater inputs shall be retained separately so the correct WUE definition can be selected for each period.
- **FR-EED-004:** The system shall create an immutable annual reporting snapshot with source coverage, estimates, exclusions, corrections and approvals.
- **FR-EED-005:** Export adapters shall validate required fields and produce the current EU or Member State exchange format without embedding credentials in the artifact.
- **FR-EED-006:** Submission attempts, responses, receipts, rejections and corrections shall be auditable.
- **FR-EED-007:** Reporting calendars and reminders shall be configurable by jurisdiction and period rather than hard-coded.

### 4.4 PUE/WUE A-G rating and official labels

- **FR-LBL-001:** The platform shall calculate separate internal PUE and WUE A-G previews using the effective rating rule set.
- **FR-LBL-002:** Every preview shall display `Non-official preview`, `official=false`, calculation inputs, rule version and legal status.
- **FR-LBL-003:** The product shall not generate or imitate the official EU label design or QR code.
- **FR-LBL-004:** The system shall ingest the official database-generated label, QR destination, issue date, validity period and source receipt.
- **FR-LBL-005:** Official artifacts shall be immutable; replacements shall retain prior versions.
- **FR-LBL-006:** A preview/official mismatch shall raise a reconciliation task and shall not overwrite either value.
- **FR-LBL-007:** The public-label workflow shall support electronic availability once the applicable rule is in force.

### 4.5 Waste heat and renewable energy

- **FR-HEAT-001:** The system shall track total rated energy input and identify the EU Article 26 assessment workflow for facilities above the applicable threshold.
- **FR-HEAT-002:** Feasibility records shall include technical and economic assumptions, local heat demand, seasonal profile, network option, costs, decision, evidence and reviewer.
- **FR-HEAT-003:** The platform shall measure or document recoverable heat, reused/exported heat, thermal power, supply/return temperatures, availability, consumer and delivery agreement.
- **FR-HEAT-004:** The platform shall calculate ERF and preserve its input-energy boundary.
- **FR-HEAT-005:** `Waste-heat-reuse-ready` status shall be evidenced by boundary heat exchangers, hydraulic loops, thermal metering and related commissioning records.
- **FR-HEAT-006:** National targets, including German EnEfG 10/15/20% rules, shall be effective-dated, jurisdiction-scoped and exception-aware.
- **FR-REN-001:** Energy records shall distinguish grid electricity, onsite generation, exported energy, renewable share and contractual instruments.
- **FR-REN-002:** Optional hourly matching shall compare consumption with eligible production or contractual energy by hour while retaining unmatched consumption and instrument provenance.

### 4.6 CDU and liquid cooling

- **FR-CDU-001:** The graph shall map facility, primary, secondary, CDU, manifold, rack and device cooling relationships.
- **FR-CDU-002:** CDU monitoring shall support state, supply/return temperature, delta-T, flow, inlet/outlet pressure, differential pressure, pump speed, valve position and heat removed.
- **FR-CDU-003:** Coolant monitoring shall support fluid type, concentration where applicable, conductivity, pH where applicable, particulate/filter state, make-up volume and sample quality.
- **FR-CDU-004:** The system shall detect leaks, loss of flow, abnormal differential pressure, thermal approach degradation, sensor disagreement and operating-envelope violations.
- **FR-CDU-005:** Alarms shall identify affected racks, devices, tenants and applications, plus available redundant cooling paths.
- **FR-CDU-006:** Operators shall see CDU capacity, current load, headroom, trend, maintenance state and redundancy state.
- **FR-CDU-007:** Setpoint recommendations may be AI-assisted, but actuator changes require an authorized control workflow.

### 4.7 Multi-tenant allocation, partial PUE and CSRD/ESRS support

- **FR-TEN-001:** The system shall model organization, tenant, subtenant, contract, space, asset, meter and allocation relationships with effective dates.
- **FR-TEN-002:** Tenant users shall be prevented from reading another tenant's topology, telemetry, subscription events, files, reports, prompts or retrieved AI context.
- **FR-TEN-003:** Direct metering shall take precedence over allocation; shared overhead shall use an approved versioned driver.
- **FR-TEN-004:** Allocations shall expose direct, shared, estimated, excluded and unallocated amounts and reconcile to facility totals within a configured tolerance.
- **FR-TEN-005:** Tenant partial PUE shall be reported as an allocation metric with numerator, denominator, boundary, allocation method and uncertainty; it shall not be labeled facility PUE.
- **FR-TEN-006:** Energy and emissions exports shall include period, organizational boundary, factor source, geography, unit, calculation method and evidence references.
- **FR-TEN-007:** Scope 2 outputs shall distinguish location-based and market-based results and retain PPA, certificate and residual-mix treatment.
- **FR-TEN-008:** Reports shall state that they support CSRD/ESRS workflows and do not by themselves establish compliance or assurance.
- **FR-TEN-009:** German colocation customer reporting shall support directly attributable annual energy data where applicable.

### 4.8 Power, BMS, digital twin and AIOps

- **FR-PWR-001:** The system shall model grid, generator, switchgear, UPS, PDU, busway, rack PDU and load paths, including redundancy.
- **FR-PWR-002:** It shall monitor voltage, current, real/reactive/apparent power, energy, frequency, power factor, harmonics and capacity where provided.
- **FR-BAT-001:** It shall monitor battery system, string, module and cell SoC, SoH, voltage, temperature and internal resistance where available.
- **FR-UI-001:** The WebGL client shall render topology, capacity, alarms and thermal/cooling overlays without requiring the complete graph in the browser.
- **FR-UI-002:** WebXR/AR views shall identify assets only after authorization and shall retain a safe fallback when recognition confidence is insufficient.
- **FR-AI-001:** AIOps and LLM inference shall operate locally in the default deployment profile.
- **FR-AI-002:** AI answers shall cite internal evidence, expose confidence, respect tenant authorization and identify estimated or stale data.
- **FR-AI-003:** AI output shall be advisory and shall not approve filings, alter locked evidence or perform safety-critical control.

## 5. Non-functional requirements

- **NFR-SEC-001:** The default architecture shall support fully air-gapped operation.
- **NFR-SEC-002:** Data shall be encrypted in transit and at rest, with externally managed production secrets.
- **NFR-SEC-003:** Authorization shall combine roles, tenant scope and asset scope and be tested at API, subscription, export and AI-retrieval boundaries.
- **NFR-AUD-001:** Configuration, topology, formulas, allocations, approvals, exports and official artifacts shall have tamper-evident audit history.
- **NFR-DAT-001:** Units, timestamps, time zones and daylight-saving transitions shall be explicit and deterministic.
- **NFR-DAT-002:** Regulatory calculations shall use deterministic decimal arithmetic and approved test vectors.
- **NFR-REL-001:** Collectors shall buffer safely during outages and replay idempotently.
- **NFR-REL-002:** Backup and restore shall be tested for graph, time-series, metadata and evidence stores.
- **NFR-PERF-001:** Deployment-specific ingestion, query, traversal and subscription SLOs shall be defined and load-tested before production.
- **NFR-OPS-001:** Data freshness, gaps, failed calculations, missed deadlines, export errors and reconciliation variance shall be observable and alertable.
- **NFR-ACC-001:** Operator and tenant interfaces shall target WCAG 2.2 AA.
- **NFR-PRV-001:** Retention, deletion, legal hold and data residency shall be configurable by data class and jurisdiction.

## 6. Acceptance gates

| Gate | Minimum evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Regulatory calculation | Approved test vectors, formula/version record, unit tests, boundary review and reproducible result. |
| EU export | Schema validation, locked snapshot, reviewer approval, checksum, submission receipt or controlled dry run. |
| Label workflow | Non-official preview marking, exact threshold boundary tests, official-label import and mismatch test. |
| Heat reuse | Applicability result, feasibility record, thermal-meter mapping, ERF test and jurisdiction rule test. |
| CDU | Sensor mapping, disconnect/gap handling, alarm tests, affected-topology traversal and capacity/headroom view. |
| Tenant reporting | Cross-tenant negative tests, allocation reconciliation, pPUE explanation and emissions-factor provenance. |
| Air-gapped AIOps | No external network dependency, authorization tests, citations and human-control boundary tests. |

## 7. Delivery sequencing

1. **Foundation:** Canonical graph, telemetry envelope, identity, tenant scope, provenance and audit.
2. **Operational domains:** Power/BMS, air/liquid cooling, CDU, subscriptions and digital twin.
3. **Evidence engine:** Quality gates, aggregation, formulas, allocation and locked snapshots.
4. **Regulatory workflows:** Applicability, EU/national exports, heat-reuse evidence and official-label registry.
5. **Sustainability and AIOps:** CSRD/ESRS evidence, hourly matching, predictive models and local Copilot.

Each phase uses the repository issue templates and must link requirements by ID. A requirement is not complete until its documentation, tests, security review, operational monitoring and rollback path are complete.
